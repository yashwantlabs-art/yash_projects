PHOHA RECIPE

Poha is a popular Indian breakfast made with flattened rice, onions, peanuts, and simple spices. It is quick to prepare, light, and full of flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups thick poha (flattened rice)
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 8–10 curry leaves
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons roasted peanuts
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 lemon
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions

  1. Rinse the poha gently with water and let it rest for about 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and add the mustard seeds. When they start to crackle, add the curry leaves and green chilli.
  3. Add the chopped onion and cook until it becomes soft and translucent.
  4. Stir in the turmeric powder, salt, and roasted peanuts.
  5. Add the softened poha and mix gently so the rice does not break.
  6. Cook on low heat for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  7. Turn off the heat, garnish with fresh coriander, squeeze lemon juice over the poha, and serve hot.

Serving Suggestion:

Serve the poha with sev, fresh pomegranate seeds, and a cup of hot tea for a traditional Indian breakfast.
